# Kayan Brand Repositioning Report

## 1. Overview
The platform has been successfully repositioned from an application store (`Kayan Store | متجر كيان`) to a comprehensive digital platform (`Kayan | كيان` — `المنصة الرقمية من كيان سوفت`).

## 2. Identity Transformation
- **Old Brand Identity**: Kayan Store | متجر كيان (Focused primarily on Android APK distribution).
- **New Brand Identity**: Kayan | كيان (Unified digital platform supporting applications, software, digital products, ebooks, templates, courses, digital files, and Kayan AI).

## 3. Changed UI Areas & Navigation
- **Header**: Updated brand wordmark to **Kayan | كيان** with the sub-descriptor **المنصة الرقمية من كيان سوفت**.
- **Main Navigation**: Expanded from app-store navigation to platform-wide navigation:
  - الرئيسية / Home
  - التطبيقات / Apps
  - المنتجات الرقمية / Digital Products
  - Kayan AI
  - عن كيان / About Kayan
- **Hero Section**: Updated headline to **منصة كيان الرقمية** with the supporting proposition covering apps, software, digital products, and AI tools.
- **Hero CTAs**: Updated primary CTA to **استعرض المنصة** (`Explore Platform`) and secondary to **استكشف التطبيقات** (`Explore Apps`).
- **Product Catalog**: Updated categories to display user-friendly Arabic labels (تطبيق, برنامج, كتاب إلكتروني, قالب, دورة, صوتيات, تصميم, ملف رقمي, أخرى) while preserving underlying database enum types.
- **Footer**: Updated branding to **Kayan | كيان** with the platform descriptor and preserved all legal/contact links.
- **Admin**: Updated admin portal branding references to align with Kayan platform administration.

## 4. Preserved Functionality & Legacy Routes
- **Kayan PDF**: Preserved exactly as the flagship application product with version, package ID, download behavior, SHA-256 verification, and release history.
- **Kayan AI**: Maintained as a first-class platform pillar with full backend gateway and model router functionality.
- **Legacy URLs**: All existing routes (`/apps`, `/apps/kayan-pdf`, `/products`, `/products/:slug`, `/ai`, `/admin`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/licenses`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`) remain fully functional without broken links or schema alterations.

## 5. SEO & Metadata
- **Document Title**: `Kayan | كيان — المنصة الرقمية`
- **Meta Description**: `منصة كيان الرقمية للتطبيقات والبرامج والمنتجات الرقمية وأدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي.`
- **OpenGraph & Twitter Cards**: Updated to reflect the broader platform identity.

## 6. Testing & Quality Assurance
